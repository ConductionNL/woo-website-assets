# Origin

The official EU icons for labelling AI-generated or AI-manipulated
content, taken 2026-08-17 from the
[European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eu-icons-labelling-ai-generated-content)
(PNG pack, all variants, renamed to kebab-case). Used to visibly label
AI-generated served files in this repository — see the README's
"AI-generated imagery" convention and
[Converting images](../docs/converting-images.md).
