# Origin

OpenCatalogi was made by Conduction at the request of the municipality of
Rotterdam, which is why it shares Rotterdam's imagery.

## Favicon

- **Original** (`source/favicon.png`) — supplied by the municipality of
  Rotterdam on 2022-11-02.
- **Served file** (`favicon.ico`) — generated by Conduction from the
  original.

## Jumbotron

- **Served file** (`jumbotron.webp`) — the same file as Rotterdam's
  jumbotron: taken 2023-08-30 from the municipality's website
  ([rotterdam.nl](https://www.rotterdam.nl/)), used as-is (no separate
  original).
